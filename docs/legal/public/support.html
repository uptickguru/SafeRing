Quick setup (seniors)

  1. Open the app → set your name and one trusted person (phone number).
  2. iPhone: Settings → Apps → Messages → Unknown & Spam → enable GMG Shield filter. Phone → Call Blocking & Identification → enable GMG Shield if listed.
  3. Android: set GMG Shield as Caller ID & spam app when prompted.
  4. Add the Protect Call widget (optional).
  5. On a scary call: turn on Speaker → open Protect → confirm → Start. Warning plays; room listen follows. Not a secret recorder.
  6. Giant HELP texts your person. Call dials them on the real phone network.

Modes

Senior — big HELP. Optional 6-digit lock so the phone stays in Senior mode.
Caretaker — family edge; Approve/Deny when SafeCall is live is human-in-the-loop.

What we never claim

  • 100% scam proof
  • Secret tap of every cell call
  • Replacement for 911
  • AI that sends money or approves strangers without a person
