GMG Shield Privacy Policy

Product of Kevin Asbury (Gulf Meridian Group / GMG). Last updated: 2026-09-04.
This page describes current product behavior for store listing and users. It is not a substitute for attorney-final wording if your counsel revises it.

Short version: GMG Shield is a family tripwire. Critical trust decisions stay with people (you or your caretaker). We design for on-device work where we can. We do not sell your data. Protect Call is not a secret call recorder.

1. Who we are

GMG Shield (also referred to historically as SafeRing) is published under the developer name associated with Kevin Asbury. Brand: Gulf Meridian Group / GMG Shield. Contact: see Support.

2. What the app does

  • Helps a senior reach one trusted person (HELP / Call).
  • Optional system filters (iOS Message Filter, Call Directory; Android call screening) to reduce unknown noise.
  • Protect Call: user-started room microphone + on-device speech cues and an optional spoken warning when Speaker is on.
  • Human-in-the-loop (HITL): SafeCall / caretaker approve-deny flows require a person; the app does not silently authorize strangers or money moves.

3. Data we may process

On deviceYour name, trusted contact name/number, family password (protected storage), filter keywords, mode (Senior/Caretaker), optional Senior lock code, local preferences.
Microphone / speechOnly when you start Protect (or similar). Used for live speech-to-text cues. We do not keep a permanent audio recording of the phone call as a product feature. Ephemeral text may exist briefly in memory.
ContactsIf you grant access, to pick a trusted person — not to scrape your whole book for sale.
Optional serverIf you use live edge features (SafeCall, optional reports, device health events): technical events, hashed or minimized identifiers as implemented, caregiver actions you take. Hosted at safering.gulfmeridiangroup.com infrastructure.
Store / OSApple and Google process install and crash data under their policies.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data to data brokers.
  • We do not claim to secretly tap the cellular audio path of a normal phone call.
  • We do not use Protect as a hidden wiretap product.
  • We do not put AI in charge of “yes, send grandma’s money.”

5. Human-in-the-loop

Where the product offers Approve / Deny / Drop / HELP, a human is expected to decide. Automation may assist (filters, keywords, alerts). It must not replace family judgment for high-stakes trust.

6. Children

GMG Shield is aimed at seniors and family caretakers, not children under 13. Do not create accounts or household setups for children as the primary user.

7. Retention

Local household data stays until you clear it (Settings → Start over) or uninstall. Server logs/events, if any, are kept only as needed for safety, abuse prevention, and reliability, then deleted or minimized per ops practice. Protect audio is not retained as a call archive.

8. Your choices

  • Deny mic or speech → Protect features that need them will not run.
  • Start over clears local household setup.
  • Contact support to ask about server-side deletion related to your device where applicable.

9. Changes

We may update this page. The “Last updated” date will change. Material changes should be reflected in the app store listing when required.
