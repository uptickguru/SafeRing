Callback Rules

Rule 1: Never Call Back

If someone asks you to call back, DON'T. Just hang up.

Rule 2: Use Your Own Number

Only dial numbers you ALREADY have saved in your contacts.

Rule 3: Call the Family

If you're unsure, call a family member you trust. They can help you decide.

These rules protect you from scammers.

⚠️

Warning Signs

🕐 Urgency

Scammers push you to act FAST. They say "you'll lose it" or "act now!"

🔒 Secrecy

Scammers say "don't tell anyone" or "this is private."

💳 Odd Payment

Scammers want payment in weird ways (gift cards, crypto, wire transfers).

🔒

Never Give

💳

Bank Account Numbers

Never give your bank account number to a phone call.

🔑

Passwords

Never give your passwords to anyone over the phone.

📧

Security Questions

Never answer security questions to a stranger.

👤

Personal Info

Never share your address or birthday with a caller.

Family Password

Choose one person you trust. They can help you when you're unsure.

Use the App to Check

When in doubt, use SafeRing to check the number first.

Ask Before You Call

Before calling anyone, ask your trusted person: "Should I call this number?"
